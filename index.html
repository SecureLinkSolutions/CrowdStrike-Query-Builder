IOC IP formatter for LogScale

Paste defanged IPv4 addresses from an advisory or spreadsheet. They are refanged, validated and turned into a search you can drop into Falcon NG-SIEM. Everything runs in this page; nothing is sent anywhere.

Indicators

One per line or mixed into text. Handles [.], (.), {.}, [dot], escaped \. and ports.
